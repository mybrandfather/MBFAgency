Any cookies or local storage present are limited to what's strictly necessary for the site or its third-party services to function — for example, session handling required by our contact-form processor.

Analytics is not currently enabled on this site. If that changes, this policy will be updated to name the specific tool and what it tracks before it goes live.

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will add appropriate cookie-consent controls at that time, based on what's actually enabled.
