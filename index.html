Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions?

What services does MyBrandFather offer?

Branding and Visual Identity, Website Design and Development, Illustration and Publishing Design, practical AI Business Systems, and Digital Products and MVPs.

How long does a typical project take?

A standard brand identity project takes 2-3 weeks. Comprehensive web design and AI system development can take 4-8 weeks depending on scope and complexity. We provide a detailed timeline before starting any project.

Do you work with startups or only enterprise clients?

We work with both. We help startups establish a strong foundational brand and handle complex, ongoing design needs for enterprise clients.

How does the AI integration process work?

We start by identifying bottlenecks in your current workflows. We then design and implement custom AI chatbots, voice agents, or internal automation systems, ensuring they align perfectly with your brand identity and business goals.

Is MyBrandFather a new company?

MyBrandFather was originally founded in 2012 by a family creative team. The original business wound down around 2015. We've since repurchased the domain and rebuilt the studio using modern design, development, and AI tools — see our About page for the full story.
