Inside the Studio

A glimpse into our creative process.

The Original Beginning

MyBrandFather began in 2012 with a belief that strong ideas deserved thoughtful design. The original studio brought together the founder's creative direction and the skills of a talented family design team — illustration, branding, and early web work, done the honest way.

Why the First Chapter Ended

Limited access to modern development, SEO, automation, and marketing resources made sustainable growth difficult. Around 2015, the original operation wound down. That's not a story we're hiding from — it's the honest starting point for everything we've built since.

Why MyBrandFather Returned

The vision never went away. The rise of modern AI tools, accessible development platforms, and automation made it possible to return with a stronger foundation. The domain was later repurchased, and the rebuild began.

A Family-Led Creative Studio

MyBrandFather is not a network of subcontractors assembled for a pitch deck. It's a real family combining creative experience, illustration, branding, publishing, web production, and AI-supported systems — the same people, working together, project after project.

The family team includes Kamran Khan (Kami), an artist, cartoonist, illustrator, and graphic designer whose archive spans character development, editorial illustration, identity design, and print work. View Kami's portfolio .

Creative Craft Meets Modern Technology

We treat AI the way we'd treat any powerful tool: useful for scale and speed, never a substitute for judgment, taste, or a human reviewing the final result. Every system we build gets a human check before it reaches a client or their customers.

What We Are Building Now

Client brand and web work, original publishing through WonderTales Club, and a small portfolio of our own products under MBF Labs — RevenueViking, LogoViking, and ZaynClock among them. Some are live, some are still in development. We'd rather tell you the truth about which is which than oversell either.

Our Principles

Craftsmanship Over Speed: We obsess over the details, from the kerning of a logo to the latency of an API.

Intelligence by Design: AI is a tool we integrate thoughtfully, not a buzzword we lean on.

Honesty Over Hype: No fabricated stats, no invented testimonials, no promises we can't keep.
