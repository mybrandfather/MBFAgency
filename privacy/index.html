Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

At MyBrandFather, accessible from mybrandfather.com, we collect only what's needed to respond to your inquiries and keep this site running reliably. This policy describes exactly what that is.

Information Submitted Through the Contact Form

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, company, current website, project type, budget range, timeline, and your project description. This is sent via Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, to our email inbox. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and, if we begin working together, to deliver our services.

Server Logs, IP Address & Browser Information

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information for every visit — IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp. This is standard infrastructure logging used for security and fraud prevention, not for tracking individuals, and we do not cross-reference it with contact form submissions.

Analytics

This site does not currently run any visitor analytics or tracking scripts. If we add an analytics tool in the future, we will update this policy to name the specific tool before it goes live.

External Fonts & Assets

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome via their content delivery networks. These services may log basic technical request data (such as IP address) under their own respective privacy policies.

Outbound Third-Party Links

This site links to external products (such as RevenueViking) and services we don't control. Once you leave mybrandfather.com, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry or, if we begin a project together, for the duration of that engagement plus standard business record-keeping.

Information Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers necessary to operate this site and respond to you (currently: Web3Forms for form delivery).

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you've submitted to us by emailing hello@mybrandfather.com.

Children's Privacy

This website is directed at businesses and adults seeking creative and technology services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this site.

International Visitors

This site is operated from [CONFIRM: jurisdiction]. If you are visiting from outside that jurisdiction, your information may be processed in a different country than your own.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy as the site and services evolve. Material changes will be reflected with a new effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@mybrandfather.com. MyBrandFather is an independently operated creative studio. A public mailing address is not listed on this website.
