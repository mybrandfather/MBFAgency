FAQ

Practical questions, clear answers.

What should a small business automate first?

Start with repetitive, rules-based work that delays customer response or consumes staff time: lead capture, qualification, FAQs, appointment requests, CRM updates and routine follow-up are common examples.

What should remain human?

Sensitive customer situations, final sales judgment, complex exceptions, high-stakes advice, relationship-building and brand-critical creative decisions should keep a clear human owner.

Can you build an AI voice receptionist?

Yes, when it fits the use case. We can design voice reception flows for lead capture, common questions, urgency identification and request collection, while clearly defining escalation and human handoff.

Do you promise unlimited AI usage?

No. AI and telephony usage create real operating costs. Recurring pricing should reflect actual infrastructure, support and usage rather than hiding those costs inside an unrealistic unlimited plan.
