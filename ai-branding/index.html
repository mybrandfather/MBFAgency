FAQ

Practical questions, clear answers.

Does AI replace the human brand strategy process?

No. We use AI to accelerate research, pattern-finding and controlled production tasks, while positioning choices, taste, judgment and final creative direction stay human-led.

Can you work with an existing identity?

Yes. An AI branding engagement can strengthen an existing brand system by clarifying voice, visual rules, content guardrails and repeatable workflows without forcing a full rebrand.

How do you prevent generic AI-generated branding?

We define the human decisions first: audience, positioning, personality, visual principles, vocabulary, exclusions and review rules. AI is then constrained by that system rather than allowed to invent the brand freely.
